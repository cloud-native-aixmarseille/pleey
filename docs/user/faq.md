# FAQ

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Game title | 160 characters |
| Game description | 500 characters |
| Question or prompt text | 500 characters |
| Answers or outcomes per stage | 2 to 4 |
| Answer text | 240 characters |
| Time limit | at least 5 seconds, default 20 |
| Points per stage | at least 0, default 1000 |
| Guest nickname | 30 characters |
| Username | 3 to 32 characters: letters, digits, `_`, `-` |
| Password | at least 6 characters |
| Private party password | at least 6 characters |
| Import file | 5 MB; `.csv`, `.json`, `.md`, `.markdown`, `.txt` |
| Party PIN | 6 digits |

## Can I add an image, a video or a sound to a question?

Not yet. Questions are text only.

## Can players type the PIN somewhere?

No. Share the join link or the QR code. The PIN alone is not enough.

## Why is "Start party" disabled?

At least one player must have joined.

## Why is "Create party" disabled?

One of three reasons: the game has no stage, a party is already running on this game, or you already host another party. End the other party first.

## I am a member of an organization but "Create project" fails

Creating projects and editing the organization require the Manager or Owner role. Ask an owner to change your role.

## Can two people host the same party?

No. Only the account that created the party can control it.

## A player lost connection

They can reopen the join link and get back in, even if late joins are off. If they stay away from the lobby too long, they are removed and must rejoin.

## I played as a guest. Can I keep my score?

Not at the moment. The **Sign in to save my score** button opens the sign-in page but does not attach the score to your account. Play with your account to keep a history.

## Which browsers work?

Any recent browser. The host screen uses the keyboard shortcuts and music, so a computer is more comfortable for hosting. Players usually use a phone.
