# Pleey User Guide

Pleey runs live quizzes and prediction games for events and communities. A host launches a game, players join from their phone with a link or a QR code, and everyone answers in real time.

This guide is for people who use Pleey. It does not cover installation or development. For that, see the [technical documentation](../technical/development/index.md).

## Who does what

| Role | What they do | Needs an account? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player | Joins a party, answers questions, sees the leaderboard | No. Guests join with a nickname. |
| Host | Creates and runs a party from a game | Yes |
| Organization member | Creates and edits games in a workspace | Yes |
| Organization owner or manager | Manages projects and members of an organization | Yes |

## Vocabulary

- **Game**: the content you prepare. A game is either a **Quiz** (timed questions with a correct answer) or a **Prediction** (prompts with outcomes resolved later).
- **Stage**: one question or one prompt inside a game.
- **Party**: a live play session of a game. Each party has a 6-digit PIN.
- **Organization** and **Project**: where games live. Every account starts with a "Default" organization holding a "Default" project.
