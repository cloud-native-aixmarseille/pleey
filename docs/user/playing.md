# Playing

You can play from any phone, tablet or computer with a browser. No app to install, no account needed.

## Join a party

1. Scan the host's QR code or open the join link they shared. It looks like `https://your-pleey-host/join/123456`.
2. On the "Ready to play?" page, choose how to join:
   - **Join as guest**: keep the suggested **Guest nickname** or type your own (up to 30 characters). **Generate name** and **Shuffle avatar** give you a new random identity.
   - **Join with my account**: if you are signed in, play under your username and avatar. The party then appears in your game history.
3. If the party is private, a **Private party password** field appears. Ask the host for it.
4. Wait in the lobby. You see "Waiting for the host to start the game..." and the host's name.

You can be in only one active party at a time. If the party has already started, you can join only if the host allowed late joins. If you were already in the party and lost the connection, you can always come back with the same link.

## Answer a question

- Tap an answer tile. On a keyboard, press `1` to `9`.
- On a computer, the tiles unlock about 3 seconds after the question appears. On a phone there is no delay.
- After answering, you see "Your answer is locked in." If the host allowed answer changes, you can switch until the timer ends. Only your last answer counts.
- Answers sent after the timer ends are refused.

## See the result

After the host reveals the result, you see the message "Correct! Well played.", "Not this time." or "No answer submitted.", the points you gained on this stage and your running total. Below, the answer distribution shows the vote count per answer with **Correct answer** and **Your pick** badges, followed by the **Live standings** with your rank, marked **You**.

## How points work

- A wrong answer or no answer gives 0 points.
- A correct answer gives the stage's points, scaled by how much time was left when you answered. Answering instantly gives the full points. Answering at the last second gives 1 point.
- When a question has several correct answers, picking any of them counts as correct.
- When a stage has no timer, a correct answer gives the full points.
- Your total is the sum of all stages.

For example, on a 1000-point question with a 20-second timer, answering correctly with 10 seconds left gives 500 points.

## Leave a party

Click **Leave party** and confirm. If you leave the lobby and do not come back within a short delay, you are removed from the party.

## End of the party

The final screen shows the podium, the full standings and **Your finish** with your rank.

Guests see a **Sign in to save my score** button. At the moment this only takes you to the sign-in page. The score is not attached to the account afterwards.
